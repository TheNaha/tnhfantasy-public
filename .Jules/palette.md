# Palette's Journal

## 2025-02-18 - Visual-Only Controls & Accessibility
**Learning:** Visual-only controls (like the resolution grid) are intuitive for sighted users but completely opaque to screen readers without descriptive labels.
**Action:** Always pair visual representations (icons, mini-charts) with `aria-label` or `title` attributes that describe the *outcome* of the action, not just the visual appearance.
